## 👋 Hi, I’m Jameson

I build **internal tools and AI-assisted systems** for sales and operations teams.

My focus is **GTM engineering and applied AI**: connecting business context, data, and software to make everyday work easier.

Sales Ops by background, systems builder by craft. I’m most energized when I can shadow a teammate, spot the friction they’ve learned to ignore, and build a tool that removes it.

> **My approach:** Observe → prototype → validate → scale or stop.
>
> Keep people responsible for consequential decisions, and use evidence to decide what to automate next.

---

### 🛠️ What I build

- **Internal tools:** Search, intake flows, and small apps that remove operational bottlenecks.
- **AI-assisted workflows:** Email classification, structured drafts, and automation with human review.
- **Plugins and integrations:** Extensions that give AI assistants new capabilities and connect teams’ existing tools, data, and workflows.
